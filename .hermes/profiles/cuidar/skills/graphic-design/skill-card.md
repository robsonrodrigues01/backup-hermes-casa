## Use Case:

Designers, students, educators, and non-designers use this skill for graphic design critique, production guidance, accessibility checks, and practical recommendations across print and digital work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Design guidance may omit project-specific production requirements such as print bleed, CMYK setup, or accessibility contrast.

Mitigation: Verify final specifications against the production channel, printer or platform requirements, brand guidelines, and accessibility standards before delivery.

Risk: Tool and template recommendations may not fit professional or high-stakes design work.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as starting points and involve a qualified designer or production specialist for logos, brand identity, print production, and large-scale materials.
